About Us

Our Story

Skyline Moving was founded in May of 2025 by two college students from Omaha, Nebraska. What started as a side hustle while attending school quickly grew into a full-time business thanks to our strong work ethic, affordable pricing, and commitment to customer satisfaction. With 8+ combined years of moving experience, we understand how important it is to handle every move with care, efficiency, and professionalism. From local apartment moves to long-distance relocations, our goal is to make the moving process as stress-free as possible. At Skyline Moving, we take pride in being reliable, hardworking, and detail-oriented. We treat every customer’s belongings like our own and always come prepared with the proper equipment, padding, and wrapping needed to get the job done safely.
